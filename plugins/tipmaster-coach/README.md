# TipMaster Coach

Play your free TipMaster soccer prediction game from your assistant. Connect
your own account through OAuth to see your competitions, open matches,
deadlines, saved predictions, results, standings and cup runs.

Ask which predictions are still open, choose your scores and confirm the
destination competitions. The included skill uses the game's ten tools to save
only the scores you confirmed before their deadlines, then check the result.
One real match can belong to several competitions, so you remain in control of
where each prediction is saved. Available modes follow your account; Classic
prediction saving stays unavailable unless the live game explicitly enables it.

The connection reads your game and sends confirmed scores to TipMaster.
Opponents' predictions stay private until lock. Results show football scores and
plain-language outcomes. The package stores no credentials or game data itself.
You can disconnect the assistant in your TipMaster account. There are no
payment, email-changing or account-deletion tools.

For public group forecasts and measured accuracy, use the separate
**TipMaster Expert Soccer Predictions** plugin.
